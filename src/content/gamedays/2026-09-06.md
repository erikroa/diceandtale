Once a month Dice & Tale takes over a venue in Berlin. This time it is Another Country
Bookshop, in the basement, from eleven in the morning until the last table finishes.

Eight tables run in parallel across three time slots. Different systems, different GMs,
different stories. Some are built for people who have never held a d20. Others go darker.
You can play one and go home, or stay for three.

Entry is donation-based. Register, pick a table, turn up.
